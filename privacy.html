Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Summary Ultrasonic Generator generates sound locally on your device. We do not record, store, or transmit any audio. We can help you exercise your data rights by forwarding requests to those providers.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how BNeoTech ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Ultrasonic Generator mobile application (the "App") available on Google Play.

About BNeoTech "BNeoTech" is a trade name (nama dagang) used by an individual developer based in Indonesia. It is not a separate legal entity such as a PT, CV, or corporation. All references to "we", "us", or "our" in this document refer to that individual developer personally.

By installing and using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

2. Our Role as Data Controller

Under applicable data protection laws (such as the GDPR or Indonesia's PDP Law), BNeoTech acts as the data controller — meaning we determine why and how information is processed in connection with the App.

However, BNeoTech does not operate its own servers or databases. The actual storage and processing of personal data is carried out by our trusted third-party service providers (Google AdMob for advertising, RevenueCat for subscription management), acting as data processors on our behalf and under their own privacy policies.

This means:

  • We do not directly hold or have direct access to your personal data on our own systems.
  • We can help you exercise your rights by forwarding your request to the relevant provider.
  • You may also contact those providers directly — see sections 6 and 10.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information collected automatically

When you use the App, certain information is collected automatically by us and by our third-party service providers:

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language, region, screen size.
  • Advertising ID — the Android Advertising ID (AAID), used by Google AdMob to serve ads. You can reset or delete this ID in your device settings.
  • IP address — collected by Google AdMob and RevenueCat for fraud prevention and analytics.
  • App usage data — sessions, crash logs, and performance data collected by our service providers.

3.2 Information you provide

The App does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information directly.

If you contact us by email (bneotech.id@gmail.com), we will receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We retain this only for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

3.3 Information stored locally on your device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Your saved frequency presets and generator configurations.
  • App preferences (volume, max frequency limit, waveform settings).
  • Safety consent acknowledgment state.

This data is stored using Android's local storage and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

4. Audio Data

We do not collect audio. All sound generation happens locally on your device using a Rust DSP engine compiled into the App. The App does not access your microphone, does not record audio, and does not transmit any audio data to any server.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality.
  • Display advertisements (via Google AdMob) to users on the free tier.
  • Process and manage in-app subscriptions (via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing).
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical issues.
  • Improve the App's performance and user experience.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

7. Advertising & Opt-Out

Free users see advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use the Android Advertising ID to show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your Android device settings:

Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we display a consent dialog (Google User Messaging Platform) before loading personalized ads, as required by GDPR.

Pro users do not see advertisements.

8. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing and managed by RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase history to validate your subscription status.

To manage or cancel your subscription, go to Google Play → Subscriptions.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Data collected by third-party services (AdMob, RevenueCat) is retained according to their respective privacy policies.

Locally stored data (presets, preferences) is deleted when you uninstall the App.

10. Your Rights & How to Exercise Them

Important — How data is held BNeoTech does not operate its own servers and does not maintain a database of user information. Any personal data associated with your use of the App is held by our third-party service providers (Google AdMob and RevenueCat). We can help you exercise your rights, but final action on data we do not directly control is performed by those providers.

10.1 Rights you may have

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA in California, or Indonesia's PDP Law), you may have the right to:

Because BNeoTech does not operate its own servers, most of these rights concern data held by our third-party providers. We will help you exercise them.

  • Access the personal information held about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent (for consent-based processing).
  • Data portability (receive a copy of your data).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

10.2 Data held locally on your device

Some data — your saved presets, preferences, and safety consent — is stored only on your device. You have full control over this data:

  • Uninstall the App to delete all locally stored data.
  • Or use Settings → Data Management in the App to clear it selectively.

10.3 Data held by third-party services

Personal data collected through the App (such as your Android Advertising ID, IP address, and purchase history) is stored on infrastructure operated by:

  • Google AdMob — for advertising. You can manage or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads. For AdMob data requests, see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — for subscription management. For data access or deletion requests, see RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing — for payment processing. See Google's Privacy Policy.

10.4 How to submit a request

To exercise any of your rights, contact us at bneotech.id@gmail.com. Please include:

  • The specific right you wish to exercise (access, deletion, etc.).
  • Any information that helps identify you (e.g., the approximate date of purchase for subscription inquiries).

We will respond within 30 days. Where the request concerns data held by a third-party provider, we will forward your request to the relevant provider and inform you of the outcome. You also have the right to contact those providers directly using the links in section 10.3.

10.5 Deletion of local data

You may delete local data at any time without contacting us:

  • Uninstall the App — removes all locally stored presets, preferences, and consent state.
  • In-App: Settings → Data Management → Clear All Saved Frequencies.
  • In-App: Settings → Reset Safety Warning.

11. Children's Privacy

Not intended for children under 13. The App is not designed for use by children under the age of 13. Because the App can produce ultrasonic sound that may be harmful to hearing (especially to children, who can hear higher frequencies than adults), we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at bneotech.id@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

See the Hearing Safety page for important warnings about ultrasonic sound exposure.
