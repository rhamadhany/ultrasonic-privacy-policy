This Privacy Policy describes how BNeoTech ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Ultrasonic Generator mobile application (the "App") available on Google Play.

By installing and using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

2. Our Role as Data Controller

Under applicable data protection laws (such as the GDPR or Indonesia's PDP Law), BNeoTech acts as the data controller — meaning we determine why and how information is processed in connection with the App.

However, BNeoTech does not operate its own servers or databases. The actual storage and processing of personal data is carried out by our trusted third-party service providers (Google AdMob for advertising, RevenueCat for subscription management), acting as data processors on our behalf and under their own privacy policies.

This means:

  • We do not directly hold or have direct access to your personal data on our own systems.
  • We can help you exercise your rights by forwarding your request to the relevant provider.
  • You may also contact those providers directly — see sections 6 and 10.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information collected automatically

When you use the App, certain information is collected automatically by us and by our third-party service providers:

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language, region, screen size.
  • Advertising ID — the Android Advertising ID (AAID), used by Google AdMob to serve ads. You can reset or delete this ID in your device settings.
  • IP address — collected by Google AdMob and RevenueCat for fraud prevention and analytics.
  • App usage data — sessions, crash logs, and performance data collected by our service providers.

3.2 Information you provide

The App does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information directly.

If you contact us by email (bneotech.id@gmail.com), we will receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message. We retain this only for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

3.3 Information stored locally on your device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Your saved frequency presets and generator configurations.
  • App preferences (volume, max frequency limit, waveform settings).
  • Safety consent acknowledgment state.

This data is stored using Android's local storage and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

4. Audio Data

We do not collect audio. All sound generation happens locally on your device using a Rust DSP engine compiled into the App. The App does not access your microphone, does not record audio, and does not transmit any audio data to any server.

  • Process and manage in-app subscriptions (via RevenueCat and Google Play Billing).
  • Detect, diagnose, and fix crashes and technical issues.

10.6 Data Deletion — Step-by-Step

This section is provided so you can exercise your deletion rights without needing to contact us first. It also serves as the data deletion reference required by Google Play's Data Safety policy.

A. Local data (you delete directly)

  1. Uninstall the App — automatically removes all locally stored presets, preferences, and consent state.
  2. Or, in-app: Settings → Data Management → Clear All Saved Frequencies to delete only your saved frequency presets.
  3. Or, in-app: Settings → Reset Safety Warning to reset consent state.

These actions take effect immediately. No further step is required.

B. RevenueCat subscriber record (we delete on request)

RevenueCat stores an anonymous app user ID, subscription status, last-seen time, and (for subscriptions) the Google Play purchase token. BNeoTech can delete this record because we are its data controller.

  1. Email bneotech.id@gmail.com with subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include any of the following identifiers to help us locate your record:
    • The anonymous RevenueCat app user ID, if you know it (it appears in RevenueCat's diagnostics screen if available).
    • Or the approximate date of purchase / first use.
    • Or the Google Play order ID from your purchase receipt email.
  3. We will delete your RevenueCat subscriber record within 30 days of a verified request and confirm by email.

C. Google AdMob advertising data (you delete via Google)

Google AdMob associates ad-serving data with your Android Advertising ID. BNeoTech cannot delete this data on your behalf — only Google, acting on your direct request, can.

  1. Open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on older versions).
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID to reset it. This disassociates future ad activity from your previous identifier.
  3. Optionally, disable Ads personalization in the same screen.
  4. To request deletion of data already associated with your Google Account (including historical ad-serving logs), use Google My Activity. Google's data-deletion policy applies.

D. Google Play purchase history (you delete via Google)

Purchase history is retained by Google Play under tax, legal, and accounting obligations. BNeoTech cannot delete it.

  1. Open Google Account → Data & privacy.
  2. Under "Data from apps and services you use", find Google Play Store.
  3. Follow Google's instructions. Note that Google Play may retain purchase records as required by applicable tax and consumer-protection law even after deletion from your account view.

E. Children's data

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data through the App, contact us at bneotech.id@gmail.com and we will delete any data we control within 7 days of a verified request, and we will advise you on how to submit deletion requests to Google for data they control.

Response timeline

  • RevenueCat subscriber record: ≤ 30 days from verified request.
  • Children's data: ≤ 7 days from verified request.
  • Google-controlled data: subject to Google's own timelines — typically within 30 days after your direct request; some data is retained by Google for legal obligations.
