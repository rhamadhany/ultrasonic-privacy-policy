2. Our Role as Data Controller

Under applicable data protection laws (such as the GDPR or Indonesia's PDP Law), BNeoTech acts as the data controller — meaning we determine why and how information is processed in connection with the App.

However, BNeoTech does not operate its own servers or databases. The actual storage and processing of personal data is carried out by third-party service providers. Depending on the service and the type of data, these providers may act either as data processors on BNeoTech's behalf (for example, RevenueCat for subscription management) or as independent data controllers under their own privacy policies (for example, Google for advertising and payments). See section 10.3 for a breakdown of who controls what.

This means:

  • We do not directly hold or have direct access to your personal data on our own systems.
  • For data controlled by third-party providers (such as Google), only those providers can act on your request. You must contact them directly — see sections 6 and 10.
  • For data we do control (RevenueCat subscriber records), you may contact us — see section 10.6.B.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information collected automatically

When you use the App, certain information is collected automatically by us and by our third-party service providers:

  • Device information — device model, operating system version, language, region, screen size.
  • Advertising ID — the Android Advertising ID (AAID), used by Google AdMob to serve ads. You can reset or delete this ID in your device settings.
  • IP address — collected by Google AdMob and RevenueCat for fraud prevention and analytics.
  • App usage data — sessions, crash logs, and performance data collected by our service providers.

3.2 Information you provide

The App does not require an account. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personally identifiable information directly.

If you contact us by email (bneotech.id@gmail.com), your email address and any information you choose to include in your message are received by us. This is retained only for the purpose of responding to your inquiry.

3.3 Information stored locally on your device

The following information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Your saved frequency presets and generator configurations.
  • App preferences (volume, max frequency limit, waveform settings).
  • Safety consent acknowledgment state.

This data is stored using Android's local storage and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

4. Audio Data

We do not collect audio. All sound generation happens locally on your device using a Rust DSP engine compiled into the App. The App does not access your microphone, does not record audio, and does not transmit any audio data to any server.

8. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play Billing and managed by RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase history to validate your subscription status.

To manage or cancel your subscription, go to Google Play → Subscriptions.

Cancelling vs. deleting are two separate actions.
  • Cancelling your subscription is done by you in Google Play → Subscriptions. It stops future billing.
  • Deleting your RevenueCat data (see Section 10.6.B) is done by us, and only after the subscription has ended. It does not cancel your subscription.
If your goal is to stop paying and remove your data: first cancel in Google Play, then email us to delete your history.

10.6 Data Deletion — Step-by-Step

This section is provided so you can exercise your deletion rights without needing to contact us first. It also serves as the data deletion reference required by Google Play's Data Safety policy.

A. Local data (you delete directly)

  1. Uninstall the App — automatically removes all locally stored presets, preferences, and consent state.
  2. Or, in-app: Settings → Data Management → Clear All Saved Frequencies to delete only your saved frequency presets.
  3. Or, in-app: Settings → Reset Safety Warning to reset consent state.

These actions take effect immediately. No further step is required.

B. RevenueCat subscriber record

What we store, and why. To manage and validate your Pro subscription, we use a third-party service called RevenueCat. RevenueCat stores the following data on our behalf:

  • An anonymous app user ID — a random identifier assigned per installation. It is not your name, email, or Google Account.
  • Your subscription status (active, expired, in trial, etc.).
  • The Google Play purchase token — an internal receipt from Google Play that proves your purchase. This is not your card number; your card data never reaches us or RevenueCat.
  • The last time you opened the App with that subscription linked.

This data is necessary for the App to know whether to unlock Pro features for you. Without it, we cannot provide the Pro service you paid for.

When can this data be deleted? When your subscription is no longer active — either because it expired on its own, or because you cancelled it in Google Play → Subscriptions. Once the subscription is inactive, this record serves no purpose and you can ask us to delete it.
If your subscription is still active

You cannot permanently delete this record while you are still subscribed. This is not our choice — it is a technical requirement of how subscription validation works:

  • The record is what proves to the App that you are a paying Pro user. Deleting it while the subscription is active would temporarily lock you out of Pro features.
  • Even if we delete the record, the next time you open the App, the App will re-create it automatically from your still-valid Google Play purchase receipt. So the deletion would not be permanent anyway.

If you simply want to stop using Pro, cancel your subscription in Google Play → Subscriptions. Once Google confirms the cancellation, the record becomes eligible for deletion (see next section).

Note: cancelling your subscription and requesting deletion of your RevenueCat data are two separate actions. Cancelling is done by you in Google Play. Deletion requests can only be handled after the subscription has ended.

If your subscription has ended and you want your history deleted
  1. Confirm your subscription is inactive — either it expired naturally, or you cancelled it in Google Play → Subscriptions (a confirmation email is typically sent by Google).
  2. Email us at bneotech.id@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my subscription history".
  3. In the email, include any identifier that helps us find your record: the anonymous RevenueCat app user ID (if you have it), the approximate date of your first purchase, or the Google Play order ID from your purchase receipt.
  4. Upon a verified request, your RevenueCat subscriber record is removed from our active records, and a confirmation is sent to the email address provided.

C. Google AdMob advertising data (you delete via Google)

Google AdMob associates ad-serving data with your Android Advertising ID. BNeoTech cannot delete this data on your behalf — only Google, acting on your direct request, can.

  1. Open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads on older versions).
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID to reset it. This disassociates future ad activity from your previous identifier.
  3. Optionally, disable Ads personalization in the same screen.
  4. To request deletion of data already associated with your Google Account (including historical ad-serving logs), use Google My Activity. Google's data-deletion policy applies.

D. Google Play purchase history (you delete via Google)

Purchase history is retained by Google Play. BNeoTech cannot delete it.

  1. Open Google Account → Data & privacy.
  2. Under "Data from apps and services you use", find Google Play Store.
  3. Follow Google's instructions.

For data held by Google, use the self-service steps in sections 10.6.C and 10.6.D above to submit a deletion request to Google directly.

Subscription cancellation is separate. None of the deletion actions above cancel your Google Play subscription or issue a refund. To stop billing, cancel in Google Play → Subscriptions first.
